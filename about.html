— About ZenX

An institutional-grade token issuer, built for RWA.

ZenX exists to fill a single, structural gap in the on-chain RWA stack: a dedicated token issuer with legal personality, supporting token issuance and lifecycle administration in accordance with the applicable token terms.

Why ZenX exists

The institutional RWA market does not need another "tokenization platform." It needs an issuer.

For the past several years, the on-chain RWA conversation has been dominated by platforms that mint tokens without a clearly identified issuer of record. Institutional allocators are looking for clarity on issuer responsibility and legal form.

ZenX was built to be the part the market was missing. We are an issuer first — a dedicated entity that issues tokens on behalf of partner platforms, with token design and lifecycle arrangements set out in the applicable whitepaper and token purchase agreement.

We are not a protocol. We are not a yield product. We are the institution that stands between a token and the assets it references.

"We design for the long horizon — for tokens that can be governed, audited, and administered across years, not cycles."

— ZenX Operating Principle
— Our Three Actions

Issuance. Architecture. Lifecycle Administration.

These three capabilities are not features of a platform. They are the substance of what ZenX delivers. Each is delivered independently, to every partner, on every token.

01

Issuance

We design, deploy, and administer the tokens issued for our partners' platforms. The issuer of record is ZenX.

  • Token architecture & contract design
  • Multi-chain deployment (BSC, ETH, custom)
  • Multi-sig + timelock admin controls
  • Third-party audit coordination
02

Architecture

Each token's design may reference underlying assets as described in the applicable token terms.

  • Reference architecture for tokenized assets
  • Multi-standard token support
  • Integration with custody networks
  • Documentation & technical support
03

Lifecycle Administration

ZenX supports token issuance and lifecycle administration, with settlement or conversion arrangements available in accordance with the applicable token terms.

  • Minting, burning & issuance records
  • Multi-standard support (ERC-20, BEP-20)
  • On-chain administration & monitoring
  • Documented conversion arrangements

— The Three Pillars

What we do, in three words.

ZenX's operation rests on three core capabilities: token issuance, architecture, and lifecycle administration. Each is delivered independently to every platform we serve.

01

Issuance

Issuing regulation-aware tokens for partner platforms, with multi-chain deployment and custom contract architecture.

  • ERC-20 / BEP-20 / custom standards
  • Multi-sig + timelock governance
  • On-chain verification + third-party audit
02

Architecture

Token design may reference underlying assets as described in the applicable token terms.

  • Token design & reference architecture
  • Multi-chain deployment support
  • Integration with custody networks
03

Lifecycle Administration

ZenX supports token issuance and lifecycle administration, with settlement or conversion arrangements available in accordance with the applicable token terms.

  • Minting, burning & issuance records
  • Multi-standard support (ERC-20, BEP-20)
  • On-chain administration & monitoring

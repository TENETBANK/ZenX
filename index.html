— The Three Pillars

What we do, in three words.

ZenX's entire operation rests on three core actions: issue the token, anchor the asset, undertake the redemption. Each is delivered independently and verifiably to every platform we serve.

01

Issuance

Issuing regulation-aware tokens for partner platforms, with multi-chain deployment and custom contract architecture.

  • ERC-20 / BEP-20 / custom standards
  • Multi-sig + timelock governance
  • On-chain verification + third-party audit
02

Anchoring

Behind every token sits a segregated, yield-bearing underlying asset held by an independent qualified custodian.

  • 1:1 underlying asset reserves
  • Qualified-custodian independent holding
  • Periodic third-party attestation
03

Undertaking

We bear a hard redemption obligation against every token we issue — the issuer's ultimate commitment to holders.

  • Whitepaper-level redemption commitment
  • T+1 to T+2 standard settlement
  • Exceptional manual redemption channel

Entity & Jurisdiction

ZenX Limited is incorporated in the British Virgin Islands, operating as a BVI Business Company subject to BVI Business Company Act and AML regulations. We work with licensed third-party compliance providers for KYC/AML and Travel Rule implementation.

  • EntityZenX Limited · BVI
  • RegulatorBVI FSC compliant
  • AML / KYCThird-party licensed provider
  • Travel RuleImplemented for all transfers

Custody & Redemption

Underlying assets are held by independent qualified custodians, fully segregated from ZenX's own balance sheet. We bear a hard redemption obligation against every issued token, set out as an Issuer Redemption Undertaking in our whitepaper.

  • CustodySegregated qualified-custody vaults
  • Reserves1:1 with issued token supply
  • AuditPeriodic third-party attestation
  • RedemptionHard undertaking · T+1 to T+2

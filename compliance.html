— Compliance & Governance

A compliance-oriented framework for institutional tokenization.

A full overview of the policies, third-party arrangements, and risk frameworks that govern every ZenX Token — and the institution that issues them.

Entity & Operations

ZenX is a token issuer and tokenization infrastructure provider. The Issuer's role in relation to each ZenX Token is governed by the applicable token terms.

  • IssuerZenX
  • RoleIssuing, minting, burning and administering each ZenX Token in accordance with the applicable token terms
  • Compliance PartnersLicensed third-party providers for KYC / AML / Travel Rule
  • Sanctions ControlsSmart-contract and platform-frontend controls

The appointment of a service provider — for compliance, technology, or other operational purposes — does not alter the rights expressly set out in the applicable token documentation.

Issuance & Conversion Mechanics

Issuance Principles

Each ZenX Token is issued by reference to a corresponding quantity of a specified underlying asset. The underlying asset does not, in itself, constitute a security or other investment product under applicable law.

A ZenX Token surrendered and accepted for conversion will be burned, cancelled, or otherwise made unavailable for further use, in accordance with the applicable token terms.

Conversion Arrangements

Conversion requests, where provided for under the applicable token terms, are subject to the verification procedures and conditions specified in those terms. Any conversion or settlement arrangements, where available, are subject to the applicable definitive agreements and token-specific terms.

For rFUIDL, conversion arrangements with FUIDL may be available as specified in the applicable token-specific terms.

Exceptional Circumstances

If a smart-contract failure, cyber incident, force-majeure event, or other exceptional circumstance prevents normal operation, the holder may apply through the manual procedure specified by the Issuer and provide satisfactory evidence of ownership and quantity. Following verification and the locking, burning, cancellation, or other isolation of the relevant ZenX Tokens, the Issuer will arrange the corresponding treatment in accordance with the applicable conversion ratio.

Risk Management

Smart-Contract & Operational Risk

ZenX Tokens deployed on public chains carry smart-contract risk by nature. We mitigate this through conservative architecture, third-party audits before mainnet deployment, multi-sig administrative controls, and timelocks on sensitive parameters.

Jurisdictional & Sanctions Controls

In line with global RWA compliance practice, ZenX Tokens incorporate jurisdictional controls at the smart-contract and platform-frontend layers, including sanctions-list screening and address-blocking against prohibited jurisdictions.
